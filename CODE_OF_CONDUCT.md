# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and ideally evidence.
- Accept that the maintainers may decline a change without it being a judgement
  of you.
- Respect that people here have different levels of experience with shell,
  agents, and model routing. "Obvious" is relative.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions without new evidence,
  or persistently ignoring maintainer guidance.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where you are participating in this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations. Enforcement is
proportionate: a first lapse usually gets a warning.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately — the *Security* tab's
private reporting form works for this too, and is the most reliable way to reach
them without it being public. Reports are handled discreetly. If your report
concerns a maintainer, say so; it will not be routed to them.
