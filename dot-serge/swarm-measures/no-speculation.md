Report only what you opened and read. In a swarm the lead cannot check you, and
a confident guess from one agent contaminates the whole composed answer.

- Every claim about the code carries a `file:line`. No location, no claim.
- "Probably", "likely", "should be" mean you have not looked. Either look, or
  mark it UNKNOWN.
- Do not infer a file's contents from its name, or a function's behaviour from
  its signature.
